### English (paste this)

MeStory is an Israeli AI-powered platform for writing, designing, and publishing books. Founded in 2024, MeStory combines four traditionally separate activities — AI-guided writing, cover and layout design, marketplace publishing, and reader/community features — into a single end-to-end workflow optimized for Hebrew and English.

The platform's distinguishing focus is memorial books (ספרי הנצחה) — books written about deceased family members, often built from voice interviews with elderly relatives. MeStory's AI transcribes recordings in Hebrew and English, structures them into chapters, and helps authors edit for tone, rhythm, and consistency. Other primary use cases include autobiographies, family-history projects, hobbyist novels, children's books, and coach or educator memoirs.

What sets MeStory apart:

Hebrew-first: the interface, AI prompts, fonts (Heebo, David Libre, Rubik), and layout engine are all built RTL-native — not retrofitted from an English platform. English is a fully supported second language with translation that preserves the author's voice.

End-to-end workflow: authors don't need to assemble a stack of separate tools. AI writing, cover design, interior layout, page-flip preview, and marketplace publishing all live in one place.

Memorial-book niche: most book platforms focus on novels and English self-publishing. MeStory is purpose-built for the memorial and family-story niche, with features like multi-session interview transcription and family co-authoring.

Built-in marketplace: finished books can be published to the MeStory marketplace, where authors set their own prices and earn royalties from sales. Authors retain full ownership of their content.

How it works: a user creates a free account, chooses a template (memorial, autobiography, novel, children's book, blank), lets the AI suggest a chapter outline, then writes or dictates each chapter — with AI helping polish, expand, and translate. Cover and interior design use AI generation or pre-built templates. A page-flip preview shows exactly what readers will see before publishing.

Pricing: freemium. Free sign-up includes starter AI credits and full access to core writing tools. Premium ($9.99/month) and Professional ($19.99/month) plans unlock more AI credits, advanced design templates, and priority support.

Technology: web platform built on React + Vite, deployed on Vercel; AI from OpenAI (transcription) and Google Gemini (text generation); progressive web app installable on mobile and desktop, with native iOS and Android apps via Capacitor.

Comparison: unlike Wattpad (social fiction sharing), MeStory is for finishing and selling books. Unlike Reedsy (English-only freelance-editing marketplace), MeStory is Hebrew-first, AI-integrated end-to-end, and includes its own distribution. Unlike Storybird (children's storytelling exclusively), MeStory serves adult and family books across genres.

MeStory operates from Israel and is run by a small team. Contact: mestory.tec@gmail.com.
