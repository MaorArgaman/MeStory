# MeStory — Directories Submission List

> 25 directories, prioritized by **impact-per-hour-of-effort**. Use as a checklist.
> Crunchbase + Wikidata are tracked separately (Sections 1 & 2 of the brief).
> Source of truth for descriptions: the Crunchbase profile draft + `/llms.txt`.

---

## How to read this list

- **Effort** = realistic submission time including account creation, screenshot upload, and waiting on form quirks.
- **Impact (Backlink)** = SEO authority of the resulting backlink. ★★★ = follow link from a high-authority domain. ★ = nofollow or low-authority.
- **Impact (AI citation)** = how often LLMs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) cite this site as a source. ★★★ = frequently. ★ = rarely.
- **Red flags** = signals the site might be a low-quality or spam directory. ⚠️ = caution; ❌ = don't bother.

## Tier 1 — Highest ROI (do these first, this week)

These are the 5 highest impact-per-hour submissions. Aim to finish all 5 within a single afternoon.

### 1. Product Hunt
- **URL**: https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new
- **Free/Paid**: Free
- **Time**: 2–3 hours (one-time launch event, see Section 4 deliverable)
- **Info needed**: Tagline, description, gallery (5 images), maker comment, video (recommended)
- **Backlink**: ★★★ (DR ~91, dofollow on the product page)
- **AI citation**: ★★★ (heavily indexed by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude)
- **Red flags**: None
- **Notes**: This is the single biggest discoverability event you'll have this year. Don't submit casually — use the launch package.

### 2. There's an AI for That

- **AI citation**: ★★★ (one of the most-cited AI-tool directories by LLMs)
- **Red flags**: None
- **Notes**: Submit before Product Hunt launch — they aggregate PH new launches separately, so order matters.

### 3. AlternativeTo

## Tier 2 — Strong ROI (do within 2 weeks of Tier 1)

### 6. Capterra
- **URL**: https://www.capterra.com/vendors/sign-up
- **Free/Paid**: Free listing; paid for premium placement
- **Time**: 45 min (the form is verbose)
- **Info needed**: Detailed feature breakdown, pricing, customer support details, demo video, screenshots, integrations
- **Backlink**: ★★★ (DR ~92)
- **AI citation**: ★★ (cited in B2B SaaS queries; less for consumer/creative tools)
- **Red flags**: ⚠️ Capterra is part of Gartner Digital Markets (with G2, GetApp, SoftwareAdvice). Once you submit to one, the others auto-populate — but you'll get sales calls.
- **Notes**: Best after you have 5+ real customer reviews. Reviews = ranking. Without reviews, the listing exists but is buried.

### 7. GetApp

- **Free/Paid**: Free
- **Time**: 5 min after Capterra (auto-syncs)
- **Info needed**: Same as Capterra
- **Backlink**: ★★★ (DR ~85)
- **AI citation**: ★★
- **Red flags**: ⚠️ See Capterra
- **Notes**: Free freebie alongside Capterra. Don't submit independently.

### 8. G2 (G2 Crowd)

### 11. BetaList

### 12. Future Tools

### 14. Trustpilot

## Total expected impact

If you complete Tier 1 + Tier 2:
- ~10 high-quality dofollow backlinks from DR 60–90 domains
- 3–5 of these are heavily-cited LLM sources (Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, Geektime, There's an AI for That)
- 1 curated Israeli directory listing (StartupNation Central)
- Foundation for Wikidata notability — at least 3 of these are accepted Wikidata references

That's the foundation. After this, the work shifts from directories to **content** (Sections 5, 6, 7) and **press** (Section 8) — which is where compounding starts.
