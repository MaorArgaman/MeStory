## 6. Launch-day playbook

> Product Hunt launches are 12:01 AM Pacific time (9:01 AM Israel time during PST, 10:01 AM during PDT). All times below are in **Israel time**, with the assumption you're launching during PDT (most of the year).

| Time (Israel) | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **T-7 days** | Schedule the launch on PH | Use a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday. Mondays are busy. Fridays are quiet. |
| **T-3 days** | DM your "soft network" with launch date | 5–15 friends + family. **Do not send the URL yet** — just "I'm launching MeStory on PH on [day]; would love your support." |
| **T-1 day evening** | Post on LinkedIn + Instagram + Facebook | "Tomorrow at 10am Israel time, we're launching on Product Hunt. Will share the link in the morning." Hebrew + English. |
| **10:01 AM** | **Launch goes live**. Post the link to your soft network DM thread. | Don't blast everywhere yet. |
| **10:30 AM** | Drop the maker comment (Section 3 above) | Pin it if PH lets you. |
| **11:00 AM** | Send the launch link via email/Slack/WhatsApp to friends + family | First wave of upvotes from your network. |
| **11:00 AM – 1:00 PM** | **Reply to every comment within 15 minutes** | This is what PH algorithm watches. Reply length: 2–4 sentences each, warm, no canned responses. |
| **12:00 PM** | Post on LinkedIn (English): "We launched on PH today" | Tag people who helped. |
| **12:30 PM** | Post on Instagram + Facebook in Hebrew | Different tone — more emotional, family-oriented. See "Hebrew companion content" below. |
| **1:00 PM** | First press email | If you have any press contacts, send a short pitch with the PH link as a "we're trending #X today" hook. |
| **2:00 PM – 6:00 PM** | Continue replying. Share to relevant communities (where allowed). | Avoid begging for upvotes — share the *story* of the product, not the link alone. |
| **8:00 PM** | If trending in top 5: post a "thank you" update everywhere with current rank | Generates a second wave of attention. |
| **End of day (midnight Israel time, 2 PM PT)** | PH closes for the day | Final rank locked. |
| **T+1 morning** | Post wherever you launched yesterday: "We finished #X for the day! Thank you" | Keeps the momentum into the next 24 hours; PH's "yesterday's leaders" page also drives traffic. |
| **T+1 to T+7** | Reply to every late comment, send personal thank-you DMs to top 10 supporters | Long tail of relationships > the launch-day spike |

### Critical rules (PH community moderation)

- ❌ **Never** ask people to upvote — this gets you flagged and downranked. Ask for "feedback" or "support" instead.
- ❌ **Never** vote-trade. PH detects pattern-matched mutual upvoting and shadow-bans accounts.
- ❌ **Don't** post the link in 50 Slack channels at 10:01 AM. Looks like spam, gets flagged.
- ✅ **Do** make the maker comment compelling — that's what people read first.
- ✅ **Do** reply to negative or skeptical comments. Don't delete or argue. A graceful reply to a critic builds trust faster than 10 fanboys.

- ☐ At least 3 demo books visible in the marketplace (don't launch with empty marketplace)
- ☐ At least 5 high-quality screenshots ready (matching gallery captions in Section 4)
- ☐ A 30-second product video uploaded (PH highly favors videos in gallery)
- ☐ Logo at 240×240 PNG ready for PH product image
- ☐ Maker (you) has a complete PH profile with a real photo, bio, and Twitter/LinkedIn linked
- ☐ The PH product page has been populated as a draft a few days before launch (PH lets reviewers see drafts; helps catch issues)
- ☐ Customer support / contact email monitored on launch day (people will email; respond within 1 hour)
- ☐ Server can handle a 5–10× traffic spike (Vercel auto-scales but check Functions limits)
